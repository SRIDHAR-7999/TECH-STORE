About SM Mobile Deals

SM Mobile Deals is the online storefront of SM Mobiles, a mobile and laptop retail showroom based in Gandhipuram, Coimbatore. We deal in genuine smartphones, laptops, smart watches and accessories from brands like Apple, Samsung, OnePlus, HP, Dell, Lenovo and Asus — along with device repair and exchange services.

What started as a walk-in showroom is now also a digital storefront, built so customers across Tamil Nadu (and beyond) can browse the full catalog, compare products, pay securely by UPI or card, and track their order — all without leaving home.
