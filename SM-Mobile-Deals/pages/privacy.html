Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SM Mobile Deals ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects your information when you use this website.

Information we collect

  • Account details you provide: name, email, phone number, delivery address.
  • Order information: items purchased, order value, delivery address, payment method.
  • Payment verification: when you pay via UPI, we ask you to upload a screenshot of your successful transaction so our team can manually confirm it. We do not collect or store your UPI PIN, banking password, or card number at any point.
  • Usage data stored locally in your browser (cart, wishlist, comparison list, theme preference) to make your shopping experience smoother.

How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To verify UPI/card payments and confirm order status.
  • To respond to enquiries made via the contact form, WhatsApp, or the AI assistant.
  • To improve our catalog and services.

Payment security

We never ask for your UPI PIN, net banking password, OTP, or full card number through this website. All payments are made directly through your own UPI or banking app. Card payments include a small processing surcharge, shown clearly before you pay.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Delivery details are shared only with our courier partners for the purpose of fulfilling your order.

Your choices

You can update your profile information at any time from the Profile page.

Account deletion

You can permanently delete your account at any time from the Profile page → Danger Zone. Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone or recovered — your saved address, order history and account details are removed and cannot be restored, even by our support team.

After deletion, the same email address and phone number can be used to register a brand new account. That new account starts fresh and has no connection to, or record of, the deleted one.

Contact us

For any privacy-related questions, reach us using the details on our Contact page.
