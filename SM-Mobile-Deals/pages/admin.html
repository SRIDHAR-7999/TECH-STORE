Razorpay Payment Link

Paste a Payment Link created from your Razorpay Dashboard (Payment Links → Create). It appears as a "Pay via Razorpay" option on the customer payment page, alongside UPI/GPay/PhonePe. Leave blank to hide it.

This is a static link, so Razorpay does not notify this site automatically when someone pays. After a customer pays and uploads their screenshot (or tells you), confirm the order the same way as any other payment in the Orders tab — it's recorded there like every other transaction.

Full Catalog Sync

Use this once after connecting a fresh database, or any time the live product count looks lower than expected. Existing rows are updated, not duplicated.
